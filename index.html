[ 00 ] hi. i'm aidan.

I work on software, machine learning, electronics, and the odd thing that doesn't fit neatly into any of those categories.

Most of what I make starts with a problem I want to understand. Sometimes that's getting a robot across a building. Sometimes it's figuring out which knobs on a synthesizer made a sound. Sometimes it's a website for somebody who needs a website.

And this is my little place on the internet. I wanted it to feel more like a website someone actually made, with pictures to look at and things to click, rather than a product demo.
